  • BranchForge

    Finds where smaller arteries leave the aorta in a CT scan, then lays them out in a custom 3D Slicer workspace. It proposes curved branch paths and cross-checks each one with CT cross-sections and small 3D neural networks that run on CPU. On the development cases it matched 17 of 19 branch origins, a 91.9% F1 score.

    Python, 3D Slicer, SimpleITK

    source
